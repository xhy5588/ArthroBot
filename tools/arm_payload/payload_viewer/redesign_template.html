      IDEAL-MODEL CHECKS

      Validation and its limits

        Common assumptions

        These are engineering concepts checked against a mathematical model. A passing result does not certify a manufactured mechanism, gripper, printed parts, bearings or support frame.

        Keep the base fixed; separate position and orientation

        Base yaw, shoulder pitch and elbow pitch position the wrist. Three intersecting wrist axes set the module orientation. A compact wrist helps orientation coverage, but the arm still needs enough span to reach the far corner. Extra shoulder and elbow reduction is a proposed transmission budget, not selected or installed hardware.

        At the far horizontal corner, the 2 kg payload alone contributes about 23.7 N·m about a conventional radial shoulder-pitch axis, before arm weight. Dividing the reach among shorter links does not remove this load moment. The ideal equal pivot spans must be at least 737 mm with the shoulder at mid-height, or 852 mm with the shoulder 50 mm above the cube bottom. The 750 + 750 mm candidate has only about 25 mm of combined outer-reach margin in the mid-height model; these are not final tube cut lengths.
