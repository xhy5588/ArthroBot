Model assumptions and map resolution

The fixed base is vertical and the object’s center of mass is at the between-jaw TCP. The existing gripper geometry is held open. The self-collision screen uses approximate spheres with a 5 mm sphere buffer. External obstacles, floor/table, and object shape are excluded; negative heights need an elevated mount.

Each point is a sampled posture, not a guaranteed optimum at every point in the surrounding cell. Missing samples do not prove a region is impossible. A pose existing does not establish a collision-free path to it. The arm drawing shows joint locations and a schematic gripper, not the collision geometry. Very large gravity-only capacities near vertical alignment do not establish structural or bearing capacity.

The length preview uses 262,144 sampled joint poses and 5 cm cells. Rod mass scales from 8 g per 150 mm; distal fittings and the rod centers move with the new length. Shorter rods may not fit the existing fittings. No collision screening, object geometry, stiffness, strength, dynamic loads, cable routing, or whole-cube orientation guarantee is included. The cube is a scale reference: its near face is 10 cm from the base axis, and its center height matches joint 1. The gripped object’s center of mass is assumed to coincide with the TCP.
